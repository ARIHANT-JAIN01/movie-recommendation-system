data/ratings.csv in this project was GENERATED by scripts/generate_synthetic_ratings.py
(seed 7, 668 users, ~109k ratings over the movies in movies.csv).

It exists only so the pipeline runs out of the box. It is NOT real MovieLens data:
accuracy numbers and recommendations from it are not meaningful as MovieLens results.

To use the real dataset, download MovieLens "ml-latest-small" (or any size) from
https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/ and copy its ratings.csv over data/ratings.csv
(columns: userId,movieId,rating,timestamp) together with its matching movies.csv. Then:
    make clean && make train
